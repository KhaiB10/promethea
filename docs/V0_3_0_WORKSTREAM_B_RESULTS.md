## Interpretation

The between-seed sample stdev (42 pcm) and the pooled within-seed sigma (51 pcm) are within 22% of each other. For a well-mixed RNG and a deterministic transport routine, these two quantities should converge to the same value as N -> infinity. Cross-seed scatter approximately equal to within-seed scatter is the signature of a clean Monte Carlo with no seed-dependent bias.

The 4-seed mean shifts 7 pcm above the v0.2.0 canonical at 200k×200 particles. This is well inside the 1-sigma envelope (canonical sigma = 16 pcm; SEM here = 21 pcm) and consistent with statistical agreement between the 50k×120 envelope and the 200k×200 canonical run.
