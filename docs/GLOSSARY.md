# Promethea Glossary

For anyone walking in cold. Reactor jargon translated.

- **BISON** — INL fuel performance code. Models what happens inside a fuel pin/pebble.
- **Breeding ratio** — fertile material converted to fissile per fissile consumed. ≥1.0 means the reactor makes more fuel than it burns.
- **CIP** — Critical Infrastructure Protection. NERC's grid security standards.
- **Cl-37** — heavy chlorine isotope. Preferred for chloride MSRs because Cl-35 absorbs neutrons and becomes Cl-36, a long-lived beta emitter.
- **Delayed neutron precursor** — isotopes that decay seconds after fission, providing the "buffer" that makes reactors controllable. In MSRs, these flow with the salt — major complication.
- **FLiBe** — LiF–BeF₂ salt. Common fluoride MSR coolant. Requires Li-7 enrichment (export-controlled).
- **Griffin** — MOOSE-based reactor physics app from INL+ANL. Used by NRC. Open source.
- **HALEU** — High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium, 5–20 % U-235. The fuel for most advanced reactors. Limited US supply.
- **Heat pipe** — sealed tube with working fluid that transfers heat passively via phase change. eVinci uses these instead of pumped coolant.
- **k-eff** — effective neutron multiplication factor. >1 supercritical, =1 critical, <1 subcritical.
- **LOFA / LOHS / RIA** — Loss Of Flow Accident / Loss Of Heat Sink / Reactivity Insertion Accident. The three canonical safety-case transients.
- **MCFR / MCSFR** — Molten Chloride (Salt) Fast Reactor.
- **MCRE** — Molten Chloride Reactor Experiment. The DOE/INL/TerraPower experimental precursor to commercial MCFR.
- **MOOSE** — Multiphysics Object-Oriented Simulation Environment. INL's open framework.
- **MSR / MSRE** — Molten Salt Reactor / Molten Salt Reactor Experiment (ORNL, 1965–1969).
- **MSRR** — Molten Salt Research Reactor at Abilene Christian University (licensed; first new US research reactor in decades).
- **NERC** — North American Electric Reliability Corporation. Sets grid reliability standards.
- **OpenMC** — open-source Monte Carlo neutron transport code. Born at MIT/LANL.
- **PWR / BWR** — Pressurized / Boiling Water Reactor. Conventional reactors.
- **sCO₂ Brayton** — supercritical CO₂ power cycle. Higher efficiency than steam, smaller turbines.
- **SMR** — Small Modular Reactor (20–300 MWe).
- **TRISO** — Tristructural Isotropic fuel particles. Tiny coated fuel kernels used in gas reactors.
- **Walk-away safe** — the reactor stabilizes on physics alone with no operator action and no powered systems.
- **Xenon transient** — Xe-135 is a powerful neutron poison that builds up after a power reduction. Operators must manage it or risk being unable to restart.
