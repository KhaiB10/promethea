# Reading List

The papers and reports that form the intellectual foundation of Promethea. Read in roughly this order.

## Foundational MSR
- ORNL-4812 — *MSRE Design and Operations Report* (1972)
- ORNL-TM-0728 — *MSRE Fuel Salt Compositions*
- Briggs, *Molten Salt Reactor Experiment Operating Experience* (1964–1969)
- Engel et al., *Conceptual Design Characteristics of a Denatured Molten-Salt Reactor* (ORNL/TM-7207, 1980)

## Chloride fast-spectrum MSR
- ORNL Pub29596 — *Fast Spectrum Molten Salt Reactor Options*
- Elysium Industries MCSFR public technical materials (Thorium Energy Alliance archive)
- Moltex Energy SSR-U Technical Description (public PDF)
- TerraPower / Southern Co. MCRE public materials (DOE)

## Thorium fuel cycle
- Hargraves & Moir, *Liquid Fuel Nuclear Reactors* (American Scientist, 2010)
- IAEA TECDOC-1450, *Thorium Fuel Cycle — Potential Benefits and Challenges*
- Flibe Energy LFTR technical white papers

## Microreactors
- Westinghouse eVinci public materials
- Radiant Kaleidos public materials
- Oklo Aurora INL public docs
- DOE *3 Microreactor Experiments to Watch* (2026)

## Simulation tools
- Romano et al., *OpenMC: A state-of-the-art Monte Carlo code* (2015)
- Permann et al., *MOOSE: Enabling massively parallel multiphysics simulation* (2020)
- Wang et al., *Griffin: A MOOSE-based reactor physics application* (Ann. Nucl. Energy, 2024)
- Aufiero et al., *Extended OpenMC for MSR analysis* (Ann. Nucl. Energy, 2019)

## ML for reactor controls
- ReactorNet — *Machine learning framework for real-time PWR monitoring* (Nature Sci. Reports, Aug 2025)
- INL "forward digital twin" microreactor program (DOE-NE materials)
- Various ANS Transactions on RL for load following

## Adjacent (continual learning, hebbian)
- Hebb, *The Organization of Behavior* (1949) — foundational
- Modern surveys on continual learning in safety-critical systems
- Hebbnet documentation (this author's prior work)
